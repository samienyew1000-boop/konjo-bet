How to Play Chicken Road?

  1. Choose your Bet Amount and Difficulty.
  2. Press Play to start the round.
  3. Press Hop (or tap the road) to advance across the road step by step.
  4. Each safe manhole increases your cash multiplier!
  5. Watch out for traffic! Cars move top-to-bottom. As difficulty and level increase, the cars move significantly faster.
  6. Press Cash Out at any time to collect your winnings before a crash happens.
